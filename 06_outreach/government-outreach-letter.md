## Introduction

Signature Sovereign Solutions LLC is a Miami-based, Delaware-registered limited liability company developing the Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor (AIA-SMCC) — a multi-node sovereign trade infrastructure program aligned with the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) policy framework.

We write to formally introduce the **Sovereign SafeTrade Program (IMEC-SSTP)** and to explore partnership, endorsement, and co-investment pathways through U.S. Government development finance mechanisms, including DFC Political Risk Insurance, EXIM Bank supply-chain finance, and USTDA feasibility grant eligibility.

---

## Programme Architecture

The AIA-SMCC connects three strategic nodes:

| Node | Location | Function |
|------|----------|----------|
| **SSTLC** (Sovereign SafeTrade Logistics Complex) | Berbera Economic Zone, Somaliland | Mineral aggregation, third-party assaying, OECD due diligence, export documentation |
| **Dubai Transshipment Hub** | JAFZA/DMCC, United Arab Emirates | Re-export, value-added processing, LBMA-compliant bullion banking |
| **BIBC** (Bihar International Bullion Centre) | Bihar, India | LBMA-pathway refining, sovereign vaulting, downstream manufacturing |

Settlement infrastructure executes through **DASP v2.0** (Dual-Anchor Settlement Protocol), integrating R3 Corda enterprise distributed ledger technology, SWIFT gpi cross-border messaging, India RTGS/UPI rails, Fnality wholesale settlement, and PAPSS intra-African payment infrastructure.

---

## IMEC Policy Alignment

The Sovereign SafeTrade Program directly supports three IMEC policy objectives:

1. **Supply chain sovereignty** — Establishing verified, traceable mineral supply chains from Horn of Africa producing regions to Indian industrial consumers, bypassing opaque intermediary networks.
2. **Financial corridor development** — Creating USD-denominated and INR-denominated settlement pathways compliant with FATF, OECD, and BIS standards.
3. **Development finance leverage** — Structuring each node as a PPP eligible for DFC, EXIM, and multilateral co-investment, consistent with the PGII (Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment) framework.
