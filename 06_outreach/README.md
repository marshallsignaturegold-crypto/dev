# 06_outreach

Stakeholder mapping, outreach drafts, and government correspondence.

This directory holds materials intended for external outreach — government agencies, institutional partners, development finance institutions, and sovereign counterparties. Content must comply with brand voice and factual accuracy requirements in AGENTS.md.

## Intended artifacts

- Government outreach letters
- DFI concept notes and LOIs
- Stakeholder engagement templates
- Meeting notes and follow-up correspondence
- IMEC-SSTP policy alignment documents

## Required ID conventions

- Document IDs: `DOC-OUTREACH-NNN`
- Stakeholder IDs: `SH-NNNN`
