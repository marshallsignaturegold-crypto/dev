Section 01

Programme Overview

The Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor (AI-SMCC) is a bilateral trade infrastructure initiative developed by Signature Sovereign Solutions LLC to establish an institutional-grade corridor connecting India's emerging minerals refining capacity in Bihar with Somaliland's documented mineral endowment via the UAE's Jebel Ali Free Zone.

The programme is architecturally aligned with the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC), announced at the G20 New Delhi Summit in September 2023, and positions as a private-sector implementation node under the IMEC framework.

Operating under the public brand SafeTrade Program, the corridor addresses three documented market gaps: (1) certified precious metals refining capacity between the Horn of Africa and South Asian markets; (2) institutional-grade compliance infrastructure for cross-border minerals trade; and (3) sovereign risk mitigation through DFC and MIGA political risk insurance coverage.
