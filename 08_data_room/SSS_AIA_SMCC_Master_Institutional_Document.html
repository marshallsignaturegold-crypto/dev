Executive Summary

The AI-SMCC v12.0 (Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor) is a private-sector, multilateral trade infrastructure initiative operating as a regulated institutional node within the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC), announced at the G20 New Delhi Summit in September 2023.

Structured under Signature Sovereign Solutions LLC (Miami, FL / Delaware), the programme delivers a DLT-enabled gold and commodities trade corridor linking Berbera, Somaliland (via SSTLC / BEZ) through a Dubai hub to Bihar, India (via BIBC), operating under the SafeTrade Program brand and DASP v2.0 settlement architecture.
