Part I — Executive Summary

The Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor (AIA-SMCC) is a multi-node sovereign trade infrastructure programme developed by Signature Sovereign Solutions LLC (Miami, Florida; Delaware-registered). The programme establishes a verified, compliant supply chain connecting Horn of Africa mineral wealth — principally gold and silver — to Indian industrial and financial demand.

The programme operates under the Sovereign SafeTrade Program (IMEC-SSTP) brand, aligned with the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC) policy framework endorsed by the G20.

Part VI — Political Risk Insurance & Governance

Political Risk Insurance (PRI) is a foundational element of the AIA-SMCC risk architecture. Each corridor node carries jurisdiction-appropriate PRI coverage from a combination of U.S. government (DFC), multilateral (MIGA), and private market (Lloyd's) providers.

DFC Priority: The DFC Political Risk Insurance program is the primary vehicle for BIBC coverage. The DFC can insure up to $250M per project against expropriation, political violence, and currency inconvertibility in OPIC-eligible countries including India.

Part VII — KYC/AML & Sanctions Compliance

All corridor counterparties, investors, government entities, and banking relationships are subject to a FATF-compliant KYC/AML workflow administered by Signature Sovereign Solutions LLC's compliance team.

  • OFAC SDN, OFAC NS-ISA, EU Consolidated, UN Consolidated, HM Treasury watchlist screening
  • PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) enhanced due diligence for all government representatives
  • FinCEN 314(a) information-sharing program participation
  • Somaliland/Somalia sanctions distinction documented and maintained
  • UAE FATF enhanced follow-up monitoring active for Emirates NBD correspondent relationship
