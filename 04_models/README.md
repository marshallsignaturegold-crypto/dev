# 04_models — Financial Models

This directory contains financial models and projections for the **SafeTrade Program / AI-SMCC**.

## ⚠️ Scenario Disclaimer

All financial models in this directory contain **scenario-based projections for planning purposes only**. Key assumptions include forward-looking price projections and policy scenarios that have not yet been confirmed:

- **Gold price scenarios** — Projected figures above current market rates (~$3,050/oz as of Q1 2026) are scenario assumptions
- **Silver price scenarios** — Projected figures above current market rates (~$34/oz as of Q1 2026) are scenario assumptions
- **DFC Modernization Act** — $205B ceiling is a proposed policy scenario, not enacted legislation
- All volume and revenue projections are scenario estimates requiring third-party verification
