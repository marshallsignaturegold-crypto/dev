## Programme Overview

The **SafeTrade Program** — operating under the AI-SMCC v12.0 (Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor) framework — is a private-sector, multilateral trade infrastructure initiative designed to serve as a regulated institutional node within the **India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC)**, announced at the G20 New Delhi Summit in September 2023.

Our programme delivers:

1. **A regulated DLT-enabled gold and commodities corridor** linking:
   - **Berbera, Somaliland** (SSTLC / BEZ) → **Dubai, UAE** (Hub) → **Bihar, India** (BIBC)

2. **DASP v2.0 Settlement Architecture** using:
   - R3 Corda (institutional DLT)
   - SWIFT gpi (correspondent banking interoperability)
   - Fnality International (wholesale settlement)

3. **Multi-jurisdictional legal structure** across 4 jurisdictions:
   - United States (parent entity — Delaware/Florida)
   - Somaliland Business & Export Zone (BEZ)
   - UAE Free Zone
   - Bihar, India (BIPPP-2025 framework)

4. **US Government engagement** via:
   - DFC (Development Finance Corporation) — Political Risk Insurance application
   - MIGA (World Bank) — Multilateral coverage
   - US-India iCET framework alignment

## Strategic Alignment

The SafeTrade Program is structured to align with the following multilateral frameworks:

| Framework | Alignment |
|-----------|-----------|
| IMEC (G20 2023) | Direct corridor node |
