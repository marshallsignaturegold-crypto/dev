## Programme Overview

The Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor (AIA-SMCC) connects Horn of Africa mineral wealth to Indian industrial demand through two purpose-built infrastructure complexes:

| Node | Location | Function |
|------|----------|----------|
| **SSTLC** | Berbera Economic Zone, Somaliland | Mineral aggregation, assaying, OECD-certified export |
| **Dubai Hub** | JAFZA/DMCC, UAE | Transshipment, value-added processing, bullion banking |
| **BIBC** | Bihar, India | LBMA-pathway refining, vaulting, bullion banking, downstream manufacturing |

Settlement executes through **DASP v2.0** (Dual-Anchor Settlement Protocol) — R3 Corda enterprise DLT, SWIFT gpi, India RTGS/UPI, Fnality wholesale settlement, PAPSS intra-African payments.
