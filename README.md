## Corridor Summary

**SSTLC (Berbera) → Dubai Hub → BIBC (Bihar)** via **DASP v2.0**

The SafeTrade Program operates as a strategic node within the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC), announced at the G20 New Delhi Summit (September 2023). The AI-SMCC v12.0 (Afro-Indian Strategic Minerals & Capital Corridor) delivers a regulated, DLT-enabled gold and commodities trade corridor across Somaliland BEZ, UAE free zones, and Bihar's industrial incentive framework.
